Data Processing

Bronze

Raw tower and call data is preserved with ingestion metadata including source file, ingestion timestamp and row hash.

Silver

Records are type-cast, deduplicated and validated. Invalid durations and unknown towers are rejected, while dropped calls and end timestamps are derived.

Gold

One row per tower, date and hour with calls, dropped calls, drop rate, average duration, peak concurrency and utilisation.

Snowflake

The Gold CSV is staged and loaded into GOLD_TOWER_HOUR for business analysis.

Business Analysis

  • Identified the worst drop-rate hour for each tower.
  • Identified 40 towers (T081–T120) exceeding a 5% daily drop rate on more than 10 of 15 days.
  • Compared drop rates across peak concurrent-call levels.
